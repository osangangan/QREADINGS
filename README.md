# QREADINGS

QREADINGS is an ongoing research project dedicated to reconstructing the conceptual organisation of Qur'anic discourse prior to translation.

Translation is treated as the final output of a structured process of reconstruction rather than its starting point.

The project has two parallel objectives:

1. Develop a rigorous, reproducible methodology for reconstructing Qur'anic discourse.
2. Apply that methodology to individual passages through documented case studies.

## Repository Structure

```text
SPECIFICATION/
CONCEPTUAL_LIBRARY/
CASE_STUDIES/
RESEARCH_LOG/
ALGORITHMS/
CORPORA/
REFERENCES/
TEMPLATES/
```

### SPECIFICATION

Defines the methodology governing QREADINGS.

It describes the principles, analytical hierarchy, and reconstruction process independently of any individual investigation.

### CONCEPTUAL_LIBRARY

A living research library documenting current reconstructions of individual roots, constructions, and conceptual patterns.

These entries are provisional and remain subject to revision.

### CASE_STUDIES

Documents the application of the methodology to individual Qur'anic passages.

Each case study demonstrates the reconstruction process rather than serving as an independent authority.

### RESEARCH_LOG

A chronological record of investigations, including successful analyses, abandoned hypotheses, methodological revisions, and unresolved questions.

The Research Log preserves the project's research history rather than only its conclusions.

### ALGORITHMS

Defines the formal execution model of the reconstruction process.

These documents specify how an investigation proceeds from Arabic text to English reconstruction and are intended to provide the foundation for future software implementation.

### CORPORA

Stores corpora and datasets generated during investigation.

These datasets support hypothesis testing and methodological validation.

### REFERENCES

Contains external reference material consulted during research.

External references function as supporting evidence rather than primary authorities.

### TEMPLATES

Provides standard templates for investigations, case studies, conceptual library entries, and specification documents to ensure consistency across the repository.

## Guiding Principles

The repository is organised around methodology rather than translation.

The Specification defines the analytical process.

The Reconstruction Engine defines how that process executes.

The Conceptual Library records the project's current understanding.

Case Studies demonstrate application of the methodology.

The Research Log documents the evolution of the research.

No internal document in this repository possesses authority over the Qur'anic text.

The Qur'anic text remains the sole primary object of investigation.

All reconstructions remain provisional and open to revision in light of new evidence.
