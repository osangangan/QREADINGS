QREADINGS Specification
Version 0.1 (Draft)

0. Purpose
0.1 Objective
QREADINGS is a methodology for reconstructing the conceptual organisation of Qur'anic discourse prior to translation.
Its purpose is not to produce novel renderings, nor to defend inherited ones, but to establish a repeatable process by which the Qur'anic text may be analysed, evaluated, and reconstructed into English.
Translation is the final stage of the process, not the starting point.

0.2 Scope
This specification governs:
Morphological analysis
Grammatical analysis
Constituency analysis
Discourse analysis
Conceptual reconstruction
Hypothesis generation
Hypothesis evaluation
English reconstruction

This specification does not prescribe theological conclusions, legal interpretations, or doctrinal commitments.

0.3 Relationship to Other Documents
The QREADINGS project consists of four independent but related documents.

1. QREADINGS Specification
Defines the methodology and analytical process.

2. QREADINGS Conceptual Library
Maintains the current state of lexical and conceptual investigations.
Entries are provisional and continually open to revision.

3. QREADINGS Case Studies
Demonstrates the application of the methodology to individual Qur'anic passages.

4. QREADINGS Research Log
Maintains a chronological record of investigations, rejected hypotheses, unresolved questions, methodological discussions, and emerging insights.

None of these documents possesses authority over the Qur'anic text.

1. Fundamental Principle
The object of reconstruction is not words.
The object of reconstruction is the conceptual organisation encoded within Qur'anic discourse.

Words are evidence.
Grammar is evidence.
Discourse is evidence.
Conceptual patterns are evidence.

Translation is the expression of reconstructed conceptual organisation in English.

2. Analytical Hierarchy
Analysis proceeds from larger structures toward smaller structures.
The hierarchy is fixed.

Discourse
Speech Act
Clause
Construction
Grammatical Relations
Conceptual Relations
Lexical Items
Roots

Interpretation shall not begin below the highest level that remains unresolved.

If discourse remains unresolved, lexical reconstruction shall be suspended.
If clause boundaries remain unresolved, lexical reconstruction shall be suspended.
If grammatical attachment remains unresolved, semantic reconstruction shall be suspended.

3. Core Axioms
Axiom 1
Grammar precedes interpretation.
No semantic proposal may contradict the grammatical structure of the text.

Axiom 2
Discourse precedes lexical reconstruction.
Individual words shall not be interpreted independently of the discourse in which they participate.

Axiom 3
The Qur'an is its own primary semantic environment.
Lexicons, commentaries, and later traditions constitute secondary evidence.
They may inform investigation but shall not determine reconstruction.

Axiom 4
Conceptual relations take precedence over lexical substitution.
The objective is not to replace Arabic words with English words.
The objective is to reconstruct the conceptual relations encoded by the Arabic.

Axiom 5
Ambiguity shall be preserved where the Arabic preserves ambiguity.
The methodology shall not resolve uncertainty unless compelled by evidence.

Axiom 6
Every reconstruction remains falsifiable.
Each proposal must identify:
the evidence that supports it;
the evidence that challenges it;
the evidence that would require its revision.

4. Reconstruction Pipeline
Every investigation shall proceed through the following stages.

Stage 1. Morphological Analysis
↓
Stage 2. Grammatical Parsing
↓
Stage 3. Constituency Analysis
↓
Stage 4. Clause Boundary Detection
↓
Stage 5. Speech Act Identification
↓
Stage 6. Discourse Structure Mapping
↓
Stage 7. Conceptual Relation Extraction
↓
Stage 8. Corpus Investigation
↓
Stage 9. Hypothesis Generation
↓
Stage 10. Hypothesis Elimination
↓
Stage 11. English Reconstruction

No stage may be skipped without explicit justification.
The output of one stage becomes the input of the next.
Translation is the final product of the reconstruction process, not its starting point.
