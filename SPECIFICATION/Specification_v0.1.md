# QREADINGS Specification

**Version 0.2 (Draft)**

---

# 0. Purpose

## 0.1 Objective

QREADINGS is a methodology for reconstructing the conceptual organisation encoded within Qur'anic discourse prior to translation.

Its objective is not to produce novel renderings, nor to defend inherited ones, but to establish a repeatable and falsifiable process by which the Qur'anic text may be analysed, evaluated, and reconstructed into English.

Translation is the final stage of the reconstruction process, not its starting point.

---

## 0.2 Scope

This specification governs:

* Morphological analysis
* Grammatical analysis
* Constituency analysis
* Clause segmentation
* Discourse analysis
* Conceptual reconstruction
* Hypothesis generation
* Hypothesis evaluation
* English reconstruction

This specification does not prescribe theological conclusions, legal interpretations, or doctrinal commitments.

---

## 0.3 Related Documents

The QREADINGS project consists of four independent but related documents.

### QREADINGS Specification

Defines the methodology.

### QREADINGS Conceptual Library

Maintains the current state of lexical and conceptual investigations.

Entries are provisional and remain open to revision.

### QREADINGS Case Studies

Demonstrates application of the methodology to individual passages.

### QREADINGS Research Log

Records investigations, discarded hypotheses, unresolved questions, methodological discussions, and emerging ideas.

None of these documents possesses authority over the Qur'anic text.

The Qur'anic text remains the sole primary object of investigation.

# 1. Fundamental Principle

The object of reconstruction is not individual words.

The object of reconstruction is the conceptual organisation encoded within Qur'anic discourse.

Words are evidence.

Grammar is evidence.

Discourse is evidence.

Recurring Qur'anic patterns are evidence.

Translation is the expression of the reconstructed conceptual organisation in English.

---

# 2. Analytical Hierarchy

Analysis proceeds from larger structures toward smaller structures.

Interpretation shall not begin below the highest unresolved level.

The analytical hierarchy is:

1. Discourse
2. Speech Act
3. Clause
4. Construction
5. Grammatical Relations
6. Conceptual Relations
7. Lexical Items
8. Roots

If discourse remains unresolved, lower levels shall not be finalised.

If clause boundaries remain unresolved, lexical reconstruction shall remain provisional.

If grammatical attachment remains unresolved, semantic reconstruction shall remain provisional.

---

# 3. Core Axioms

## Axiom 1

Grammar precedes interpretation.

No semantic proposal may contradict the grammatical structure of the text.

---

## Axiom 2

Discourse precedes lexical reconstruction.

Individual words shall not be interpreted independently of the discourse in which they participate.

---

## Axiom 3

The Qur'an is its own primary semantic environment.

Lexicons, commentaries, and later traditions constitute secondary evidence.

They may inform investigation but shall not determine reconstruction.

---

## Axiom 4

Conceptual relations take precedence over lexical substitution.

The objective is not to replace Arabic words with English words.

The objective is to reconstruct the conceptual relations encoded by the Arabic.

---

## Axiom 5

Ambiguity shall be preserved where the Arabic preserves ambiguity.

The methodology shall not resolve uncertainty unless compelled by evidence.

---

## Axiom 6

Every reconstruction remains falsifiable.

Every proposal shall identify:

* supporting evidence;
* competing evidence;
* conditions under which the proposal would require revision.

---

# 4. Reconstruction Pipeline

Every investigation proceeds through the following stages.

1. Morphological Analysis
2. Grammatical Parsing
3. Constituency Analysis
4. Clause Boundary Detection
5. Speech Act Identification
6. Discourse Structure Mapping
7. Conceptual Relation Reconstruction
8. Corpus Investigation
9. Hypothesis Generation
10. Hypothesis Evaluation
11. English Reconstruction

No stage may be skipped without explicit justification.

The output of one stage becomes the input of the next.

Translation is the final product of reconstruction.

---

# 5. Primitive Objects

## 5.1 Text

The Qur'anic text under investigation.

The Text is the only immutable object recognised by the methodology.

---

## 5.2 Discourse

A coherent communicative event composed of one or more related speech acts.

Discourse boundaries are determined by the text itself rather than verse numbering or traditional thematic divisions.

---

## 5.3 Speech Act

A communicative action performed within a discourse.

Examples include declaration, command, question, response, clarification, warning, promise, bequest, and conclusion.

Speech Acts are functional rather than grammatical.

---

## 5.4 Clause

The smallest grammatical unit capable of expressing a proposition or communicative relation.

Clause boundaries are established through grammatical analysis.

---

## 5.5 Construction

A recurring grammatical configuration whose contribution to meaning exceeds that of its individual lexical items.

Examples include conditional constructions, detached accusatives, nominal clauses, and elative plus tamyīz constructions.

---

## 5.6 Grammatical Relation

A structural relationship established by Arabic grammar.

Examples include subject, predicate, object, modifier, tamyīz, circumstantial expression, and iḍāfah.

Grammatical relations describe structure.

They do not independently determine interpretation.

---

## 5.7 Conceptual Relation

A relationship between concepts encoded through the interaction of grammar and discourse.

Conceptual relations are reconstructed from the text.

They are not equivalent to dictionary definitions.

---

## 5.8 Lexical Item

A word occurring within the text.

Lexical items do not possess fixed meanings independent of discourse.

Their contribution must be reconstructed within their grammatical and conceptual environment.

---

## 5.9 Root

The morphological root associated with one or more lexical items.

Roots provide evidence for reconstruction.

Root investigation shall never override grammar or discourse.

---

## 5.10 Hypothesis

A proposed reconstruction intended to explain one or more observable features of the text.

Every hypothesis must:

* explain the available evidence;
* remain grammatically possible;
* remain discourse coherent;
* remain open to revision.

---

## 5.11 Evidence

Any observable feature capable of supporting or challenging a hypothesis.

Primary evidence includes:

* grammar;
* discourse;
* recurring Qur'anic usage;
* constructional patterns;
* internal textual relationships.

External literature constitutes secondary evidence.

---

## 5.12 Reconstruction

The current best explanatory model produced by applying the QREADINGS methodology.

Every reconstruction remains provisional.

No reconstruction is exempt from future revision.

---

# 6. Status of Methodological Claims

QREADINGS distinguishes between different levels of methodological confidence.

## Observation

A feature of the text requiring investigation.

## Hypothesis

A proposed explanation currently under investigation.

## Working Principle

A hypothesis that has survived repeated testing and currently guides analysis.

## Specification

A methodological rule adopted by the project.

## Deprecated

A proposal retained for historical reference but no longer employed by the methodology.

Methodological advancement proceeds by evidence rather than preference.

No proposal shall advance in status without sustained investigation.
