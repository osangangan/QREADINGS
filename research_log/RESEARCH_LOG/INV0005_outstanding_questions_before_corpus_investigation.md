# Title

Outstanding Questions as a Prerequisite for Corpus Investigation

---

# Context

During the application of the QREADINGS Reconstruction Engine to Qur'an 15:23, it became apparent that Corpus Investigation cannot proceed meaningfully without first identifying the specific analytical questions that require evidence.

The current Reconstruction Engine transitions directly from:

Conceptual Relation Reconstruction

↓

Corpus Investigation

However, the investigation naturally paused to formulate the questions that the corpus was expected to answer.

---

# Observation

Corpus Investigation is not a goal in itself.

It is an evidence-gathering activity performed in response to one or more unresolved analytical questions.

Without clearly stated questions, the scope of the corpus investigation becomes undefined.

For example, investigating the lexical root *w-r-th* could involve any of the following:

- lexical distribution;
- grammatical behaviour;
- conceptual operations;
- recurring constructions;
- discourse environments;
- relationships with neighbouring concepts;
- diachronic or synchronic patterns.

Each represents a different investigation and requires different evidence.

---

# Evidence from Case Study 15:23

Before investigating the corpus, the following questions naturally emerged:

1. What conceptual operation is encoded by **نحيي** within this discourse?

2. What conceptual operation is encoded by **نميت** within this discourse?

3. What conceptual operation is encoded by **الوارثون** within this discourse?

4. How does the concluding nominal clause relate conceptually to the two preceding verbal processes?

5. Does recurring Qur'anic usage support the current discourse reconstruction, or suggest an alternative conceptual organisation?

These questions determined the direction and scope of the subsequent corpus investigation.

---

# Competing Hypotheses

## Hypothesis A

Question formulation is already an implicit part of Corpus Investigation.

No modification to the Reconstruction Engine is required.

---

## Hypothesis B

Question formulation constitutes a distinct analytical activity that precedes Corpus Investigation.

Corpus Investigation should begin only after the required evidence has been explicitly identified.

---

# Current Decision

No change shall be made to the QREADINGS Specification or the Reconstruction Engine.

This observation shall remain under investigation until similar behaviour has been observed across multiple independent case studies.

---

# Future Validation

Future investigations should monitor whether they naturally begin Corpus Investigation by first identifying:

- unresolved analytical questions;
- evidence requirements;
- scope of investigation;
- criteria for evaluating the collected evidence.

If this pattern consistently recurs, consideration should be given to recognising "Outstanding Questions" or "Evidence Planning" as an explicit component of the Reconstruction Engine.

---

# Notes

This observation concerns the execution of the methodology rather than the interpretation of the Qur'anic text.

It suggests that Corpus Investigation is fundamentally question-driven rather than search-driven.

Accordingly, the formulation of analytical questions may represent a necessary bridge between Conceptual Relation Reconstruction and Corpus Investigation, although further evidence is required before any methodological change is proposed.
