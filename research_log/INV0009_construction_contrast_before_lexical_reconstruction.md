# Title

Construction Contrast as a Source of Conceptual Constraints

---

# Motivation

During investigation of Qur'an 90:11-13 (فك رقبة), the Reconstruction Engine encountered an important methodological situation.

The lexical family ف-ك-ك has extremely limited distribution within the Qur'anic corpus, making ordinary corpus-based lexical reconstruction difficult.

Instead of attempting to reconstruct فك in isolation, comparison with the recurring construction تحرير رقبة suggested an alternative analytical strategy.

This note records that methodological observation.

---

# Observation

The participant رقبة remains constant across four Qur'anic constructions.

Three occurrences use:

> تحرير رقبة

One occurrence uses:

> فك رقبة

Rather than immediately reconstructing the participant (رقبة), the variation between the governing constructions appears to provide stronger evidence.

In effect, the Qur'an performs a controlled comparison:

Invariant:

> رقبة

Variable:

> تحرير
>
> فك

This shifts analytical attention from isolated lexical meaning toward the conceptual distinction preserved by the competing constructions.

---

# Working Hypothesis

When a conceptual participant remains invariant across multiple constructions, the Reconstruction Engine should first investigate the contrasting constructions before attempting detailed reconstruction of the invariant participant.

Expressed procedurally:

1. Identify invariant participant(s).
2. Identify competing constructions.
3. Reconstruct the conceptual distinction preserved by the competing constructions.
4. Return to reconstruction of the participant in light of those constraints.

---

# Rationale

Variation frequently carries more explanatory information than invariance.

If multiple lexical families govern the same participant, the preservation of distinct constructions suggests that the discourse maintains a conceptual distinction between the governing operations.

Reconstructing this distinction may reduce the hypothesis space more effectively than investigating either lexical family independently.

# Current Status

The hypothesis remains unvalidated.

It has been observed in a single construction family centred on رقبة.

Future investigations should examine unrelated passages to determine whether construction-family comparison consistently reduces lexical uncertainty before lexical reconstruction begins.

---

# Questions for Future Investigation

- Does this pattern occur elsewhere in the Qur'an?
- Does construction-family comparison consistently reduce hypothesis space?
- Can this procedure replace some forms of early corpus-wide lexical investigation?
- Should "Construction Family" become an explicit analytical object within the Reconstruction Engine?

Until replicated across independent case studies, this proposal should remain a methodological hypothesis.
