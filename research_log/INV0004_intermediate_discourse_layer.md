# Title

Possible Intermediate Analytical Layer Between Speech Act Identification and Discourse Structure Mapping

---

# Context

During the application of the QREADINGS Reconstruction Engine to Qur'an 15:23, an apparent analytical activity emerged between Speech Act Identification and Discourse Structure Mapping.

The current Reconstruction Pipeline proceeds directly from:

Speech Act Identification

↓

Discourse Structure Mapping

However, the investigation appeared to require an intermediate analysis concerned with organising the relationships among identified speech acts before reconstructing the discourse as a whole.

---

# Observation

The investigation naturally proceeded through the following sequence:

1. Morphological Analysis
2. Grammatical Parsing
3. Speech Act Identification
4. Analysis of relationships among the identified speech acts
5. Discourse Structure Mapping

Step 4 appeared analytically distinct from both Speech Act Identification and Discourse Structure Mapping.

The activity was not concerned with identifying new speech acts, nor with reconstructing the complete discourse. Rather, it involved determining how the identified speech acts relate to one another and whether they form a progression, contrast, climax, conclusion, or other rhetorical organisation.

---

# Competing Hypotheses

## Hypothesis A

The current Discourse Structure Mapping stage already encompasses this activity.

No modification to the Reconstruction Engine is required.

---

## Hypothesis B

The Reconstruction Engine contains an implicit analytical stage that has not yet been formally recognised.

This stage concerns the organisation of relationships among speech acts prior to full discourse reconstruction.

---

# Current Decision

No change shall be made to either the QREADINGS Specification or the Reconstruction Engine.

The observation shall remain under investigation until it has been independently observed across multiple unrelated case studies.

---

# Future Validation

Future investigations should monitor for recurring evidence of:

- analysis of relationships among speech acts;
- rhetorical organisation preceding discourse reconstruction;
- transitions between communicative actions;
- structural progression among speech acts.

If this analytical activity consistently recurs, its status may be elevated from Observation to Hypothesis and reconsidered for inclusion in a future revision of the Reconstruction Engine.
