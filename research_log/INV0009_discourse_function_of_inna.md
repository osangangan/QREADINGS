# Title

The Discourse Function of إِنَّ (inna)

---

# Context

During the translation investigation of 15:23, difficulty arose in selecting an English rendering for the particle **inna**.

Conventional translations such as:

- indeed;
- surely;
- verily;
- truly;

appeared to function as English stylistic approximations rather than direct reconstructions of the particle's contribution.

Unlike previously investigated lexical families, **inna** is not a lexical root but a discourse particle.

This suggests that its reconstruction may require discourse analysis rather than lexical reconstruction.

---

# Research Question

Does **inna** contribute lexical meaning, or does it perform a discourse operation within Qur'anic communication?

---

# Preliminary Observations

## Observation 1

Removing **inna** from a proposition does not alter its propositional content.

The factual content remains unchanged.

This suggests that **inna** contributes no independent lexical content.

---

## Observation 2

The presence of **inna** alters the presentation of the proposition rather than the proposition itself.

Its contribution therefore appears to operate at the level of discourse.

---

## Observation 3

In 15:21-25, only verse 15:23 contains **inna** despite neighbouring verses making equally significant theological assertions.

This suggests that **inna** cannot be explained solely as a marker of importance or truthfulness.

---

## Observation 4

The discourse environment of 15:23 differs structurally from the surrounding verses.

Where neighbouring verses describe divine actions, 15:23 functions as a central theological declaration.

Whether this distinction is related to the presence of **inna** remains to be investigated.

---

## Observation 5

In 15:23, **inna** occurs together with additional discourse elements:

- la;
- the independent pronoun *naḥnu*.

Their individual contributions remain undetermined.

No assumption should therefore be made that all three perform the same discourse function.

---

# Working Hypotheses

## Hypothesis A

**Inna** functions primarily as an emphatic particle.

Traditional English renderings such as "indeed" or "surely" adequately capture its contribution.

---

## Hypothesis B

**Inna** functions primarily as a discourse operator.

Its contribution concerns the organisation or framing of propositions rather than lexical emphasis.

English renderings should therefore be determined by discourse function rather than by fixed lexical substitution.

---

# Current Decision

No English rendering of **inna** shall be adopted at this stage.

Further investigation should precede any lexicalisation.

---

# Methodological Decisions

During this investigation:

- traditional English glosses shall be suspended;
- discourse behaviour shall be examined prior to translation;
- local discourse context shall take precedence over isolated occurrences;
- the functions of **inna**, **la**, and independent pronouns shall be investigated separately.

---

# Future Validation

Future investigation should examine every occurrence of **inna** within Surah 15 before expanding to the wider Qur'anic corpus.

Particular attention should be given to:

- discourse transitions;
- explanatory statements;
- theological declarations;
- commands;
- narratives;
- dialogues;
- consolation passages.

The investigation should determine whether recurring discourse environments consistently predict the occurrence of **inna**.

---

# Notes

This investigation represents the first QREADINGS study directed primarily toward reconstructing a discourse particle rather than a lexical family.

If successful, it may establish a complementary methodology for discourse reconstruction alongside the existing framework for lexical reconstruction.

No modification to the Translation Specification should be made until sufficient corpus evidence has been collected.
