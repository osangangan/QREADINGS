# Divine Designation Corpus Catalogue (Stage 1)

This directory contains the descriptive empirical catalogue of direct divine designation events in the Qur me'anic discourse, constructed in accordance with Stage 1 of the QREADINGS methodology.

## Schema & Fields

Each entry records a **designation event** defined by the 10 standard analytical fields:

1. **Event ID**: Sequential identifier (e.g. `DD-0001`).
2. **Verse**: Standard Surah:Ayah reference (e.g. `1:1`).
3. **Arabic Clause**: Complete clause containing the designation event.
4. **Designation**: Exact designation referring directly to God.
5. **Designation Scope**: Categorised as `Single lexical item`, `Lexical pair`, `Multi-word designation`, or `Extended designation`.
6. **Morphology**: Morphological/grammatical form of the designation.
7. **Syntax**: Syntactic position/role within the clause.
8. **Immediate Context**: Discourse environment (`Creation`, `Legislation`, `Narrative`, `Judgement`, `Supplication`, `Polemic`, `Exhortation`, `Praise/Invocation`).
9. **Pairing**: Co-occurring designation(s) within the immediate clause/event.
10. **Observational Notes**: Strictly non-interpretive structural observations.
